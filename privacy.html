Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026 | Effective Date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications, tools, and services developed and published by TSoftStudios ("we", "our", or "us").

Jump to section: 1. General Overview 2. DayFlow App Policy 3. Photo & Video Apps 4. PDF & Scanner Apps 5. AdMob & Third Parties 6. Children's Privacy 7. Contact & Data Deletion

1. General Information & Commitment

TSoftStudios is dedicated to respecting and protecting the privacy of our application users. We design our software on a privacy-by-design model: prioritizing local on-device computation, minimizing data collection, and being completely transparent about third-party SDKs such as Google AdMob and Google Play Billing.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing or monetary consideration.

2. Application-Specific Policy: DayFlow (Routine & Habit Tracker)

Offline-First Security: Your personal habits, task checklists, personal notes, and expense numbers never leave your device.
  • Data Storage: All user-generated entries (habits, checklists, diary notes, expense amounts) are stored locally in the application's private SQLite/Room database on the user's smartphone.
  • Cloud Sync: No account creation is mandatory. If an optional backup service is used in future updates, data will be encrypted and stored only with explicit user confirmation.
  • Permissions: DayFlow requests Notification permission strictly to remind users of scheduled habits and tasks at their requested times.

3. Application-Specific Policy: Photo & Video Editing Applications

  • Camera & Gallery Permissions: Used exclusively when you choose to capture a new photo or select an image/video from your gallery for editing.
  • Local Media Processing: Filters, cropping, visual enhancements, and background removals are performed locally on your device whenever hardware allows. Images are never uploaded to remote servers without your explicit prompt.
  • No Face Recognition Database: Our editing apps do not create biometrics or persistent facial recognition records.

4. Application-Specific Policy: PDF & Document Scanner Tools

  • Camera Permission: Required strictly to take pictures of paper documents, invoices, or whiteboards for edge-detection scanning.
  • Storage / Media Files: Required to read and save generated PDF documents to your local device directory.
  • Confidentiality: TSoftStudios has zero visibility into the contents of your scanned documents or converted PDF files. Everything is processed offline on your phone.

5. Advertising & Third-Party SDK Disclosures

To offer free versions of our applications, some TSoftStudios apps incorporate industry-standard advertising and analytics frameworks:

Google AdMob (Google LLC)

Displays banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / IDFA), coarse location, and diagnostic info to deliver compliant ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing

Handles in-app purchases and subscriptions. TSoftStudios does not collect or store credit card details or financial information; all transactions are processed securely through Google Play.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Our applications are general audience tools and are not knowingly directed toward children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we take prompt steps to delete such data.

7. User Rights, Data Deletion & Contact

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR in the EU or CCPA in California), you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any data associated with you. Because our apps primarily operate offline without server accounts, you can permanently delete all local app data at any time by clearing the application storage in your Android or iOS device settings.
