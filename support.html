Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my In-App Purchases?

Open the app's Settings menu and tap "Restore Purchases". Ensure you are logged into the same Google Play or Apple ID account used for the original transaction.

Does DayFlow back up data automatically?

DayFlow operates on an offline-first privacy model, saving your data to your phone's secure database. You can export a manual backup JSON file from the settings page.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?

Use the contact form on this page or send an email to tsoftstudios2026@gmail.com with your phone model, Android/iOS version, and a screenshot.
